Create a new customer account in Acme Admin

1. Open the Acme Admin login page and sign in with the demo username and password.
2. From the dashboard, click New customer.
3. Type the customer's full name in the Name field.
4. Type the customer's email address in the Email field.
5. Choose the Business plan from the Plan dropdown.
6. Click Next to go to the permissions page.
7. Tick only the View orders and Create orders permissions. Leave Issue refunds unticked.
8. Click Next to open the review page and check that the name, email, plan and permissions match.
9. Click Submit.
10. Confirm that the success page says the customer was created.
