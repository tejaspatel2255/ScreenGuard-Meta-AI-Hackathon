Create customer refund for damaged goods

1. Sign in with the demo username and password.
2. On the Orders page, open order ORD-1002.
3. Check that the order total is $240.00.
4. Click Start refund.
5. Choose the reason Damaged item.
6. Enter the refund amount 120.00.
7. Tick Manager approved (required over $100).
8. Click Review refund.
9. Check that the reason, amount and approval are correct on the review page.
10. Click Confirm refund and check the success message.
